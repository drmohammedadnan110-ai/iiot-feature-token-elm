# Figure provenance

`proposed_architecture.png` is the architecture image supplied separately by the repository requester. `paper/image1.png` through `paper/image8.png` are unchanged embedded images from the supplied manuscript; their media filenames do not establish figure-caption numbers. The extraction manifest in [results/reported/provenance.json](../results/reported/provenance.json) records their hashes.

These figures describe the manuscript and its reported experiments. They are not newly measured results. Original author rights remain with the relevant holders; the code's MIT license does not relicense these assets.

New plots under [results/executed/synthetic_demo/figures](../results/executed/synthetic_demo/figures/) are generated from the saved synthetic-demo predictions and history. Their source CSVs and scope are included beside them.
